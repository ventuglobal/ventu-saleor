THE PROBLEM

Small businesses buy at a structural disadvantage.

Less purchasing power. Limited access to supplier credit. Procurement spread across a dozen vendors. Sourcing and quoting done by hand, then several deliveries and several invoices for what should have been one order.

Traditional distribution solves part of this, and pays for it with inventory, working capital, warehouses and people. Every unit of growth costs another unit of capital. Ventu changes the architecture instead of the arithmetic.

THE TECHNOLOGY

Turning fragmented wholesale supply into AI-ready commerce.

Ventu reads supplier catalogues in any format, normalises SKUs, fills in the missing specifications, tracks stock and price changes, observes market signals, learns from every transaction and executes procurement through AI agents.

One agent reads intent in natural language and orchestrates eleven modules across catalogue, supply, search, pricing, channels, orders, fulfilment, logistics, billing, financing and intelligence. Each one carries its real status below: live, in private beta, or in development.

THE BRAIN

Agent

Private beta

The virtual buyer that understands WhatsApp and orchestrates the whole platform in seconds.

An agentic orchestration layer with native access to catalogue, inventory, pricing and logistics. It reads intent in natural language and runs each module in the right order, with full permissions and traceability. Quoting, restocking or closing a sale happens right in the chat.

01

Data & Supply Hub

Catalog AI

Live

It cleans, standardises and enriches information for thousands of products automatically.

A PIM that reads spreadsheets, PDFs, feeds and APIs, maps attributes onto a canonical per-category schema, deduplicates by part number and fills the gaps with language models. Weeks of manual clean-up become minutes.

Supply

Live

It connects to suppliers in real time to verify prices, stock and availability.

Integration over API, file or EDI that normalises every supplier into a single availability model and resolves SKU mapping. Pricing and OMS then decide on real cost and lead time, not on a stale spreadsheet.

Search IA

Live

A smart search engine that understands buyer intent and finds the exact product.

A hybrid engine of lexical search combined with semantic embeddings and reranking. It handles part numbers, equivalents, technical synonyms and typos. Indexable and SEO-ready, so it also captures organic demand.

Intelligence

Private beta

It reads purchase history and turns data into automatic replenishment decisions.

An analytics layer over the stack's events that forecasts demand and computes reorder point and quantity per SKU, factoring in each supplier's lead time and actual performance. It delivers suggested purchase orders, not a dashboard.

02

Smart Commerce & Fintech

Pricing

Live

It computes margins, discount rules and competitive prices automatically.

A rules engine that resolves price at query time by customer, volume and channel, with margin floors, repricing inside defined bands and alerts below target margin. Every change stays auditable.

Channels

Live

It syncs catalogue and offers across Shopify, Mercado Libre and several channels simultaneously.

Native connectors with two-way sync of catalogue, price, stock and orders, each channel carrying its own mapping and pricing policy from a single source of truth. Stock is reserved centrally, so nothing oversells.

Billing

Live

It issues and manages consolidated electronic invoices with the Chilean tax authority automatically.

Electronic tax document issuance integrated with Chile's SII (invoice, dispatch note, credit notes) triggered by the sale or dispatch event, with folio numbers, digital signature and acknowledgements. It consolidates several dispatches into one document and reconciles against the order in OMS.

Financing

In development

B2B financing at 30 days built into checkout with regulated partners.

Credit assessment online during the purchase flow, without redirecting the buyer out of checkout. The order only proceeds once approved. The financing partner carries the credit risk while the operation settles in cash.

03

Asset-Light Logistics

OMS

Live

It centralises, orchestrates and manages every order in one place.

It normalises orders from any origin into a single model with a unified state machine. It routes by rules to the warehouse, store or dropship supplier that best resolves each item, handling splits, backorders and reassignment on stockout.

Fulfillment

Live

Receiving, order preparation (picking and packing) and express dispatch.

A WMS that validates receipts against the purchase order, wave-guided picking, scan-verified packing and manifest-based dispatch from mobile devices. It records traceability by operator, and courier cut-off times drive the wave so same-day dispatch is met.

Logistics

Live

It assigns third-party carriers and tracks the status of every delivery in real time.

A multi-carrier layer that assigns by destination, weight, cost and delivery promise, generates labels and consolidates tracking into a single view through to proof of delivery. It records performance by carrier so rates get renegotiated with data.
